Show the map from OpenStreetMap?

Without the map, this page works entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. To show a map under the track, your browser loads map images from the OpenStreetMap tile server (tile.openstreetmap.org, run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom). The server receives:

  • your IP address and browser details, as with every website,
  • which map area is requested – that is, roughly where this recording was made.

Your recording itself is not sent. The tile server's use of this data is described in the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. This page stores your choice in this browser (no cookie); you can switch the map off again at any time.

Privacy

  • Your recordings stay on your device. A .pitlog file you open is read by this page in your browser; it is not uploaded, and exports are created in the browser as well.
  • No cookies, no tracking, no analytics. Unless you switch on the map, the page loads nothing from other servers.
  • Stored in this browser: only whether you switched on the map (local storage). Delete it with your browser's site data.
  • Map (optional, off by default): when you switch it on, map images are loaded from tile.openstreetmap.org; that server receives your IP address and the map area of the recording. Its privacy policy.
  • Where this page itself is hosted, the host may log requests to the page (for example your IP address) like any website – not your files. Saved as a file and opened locally, nothing is logged.
